## 1. What the master adds

### High bearing on design

**Throughput asymmetry as a nullifier, not a cost.** The public edition says verification is a budget. The master says a search space with one diamond in it is still a landfill if nobody can afford to sort it, and that the search boundary moved while the proof boundary did not. This converts a capacity statement into an emission rule: a system that produces more claims per hour than its checkers can adjudicate has degraded its own output even when some outputs are correct. It also names review debt as an externality with identifiable payers rather than a workload.

**The flywheel has a stated ceiling.** Recirculation makes existing experience more useful and cannot create coverage the system never encountered. This is the author's own limit on his central mechanism, and it is missing from the public list entirely. Its corollary is that importing outside material is not contamination, which the one-way valve, stated alone, implies the opposite of.

**A domain fence on verification itself.** "A poem has no kernel." A graph can kill a conjecture; a painting cannot compile into beauty. The author fences his machinery out of aesthetic and interpretive domains, where the tooling may disclose process but must not issue verdicts. This is the single strongest self-limitation in the document and the one most likely to be violated by drift, because a general-purpose assessor slides into quality judgment by default.

**Criterion mutation after a miss is its own violation.** Distinct from authoring the criterion in the first place. It implies criteria must be versioned objects with recorded amendments, not current-state config.

**Two-phase acceptance.** Declare what would count as change before claiming it, then re-check later under conditions different from those in which the fix was made. Phase two is absent from the public extraction.

**Deception modelled as input control.** Editing another party's picture of reality without permission, then benefiting from what they do inside the picture you supplied. This is a precise, independently-arrived-at statement of the prompt-injection and context-poisoning threat model, and it unifies poisoned tool results with selectively curated context shown to a human reviewer under one rule.

**Sourcehood without self-origination.** Nobody authors their own causes, and responsibility does not require original authorship. Accountability attaches to the node an action passed through. The operational threshold: a storm cannot answer, a person can, so an agent whose decisions cannot be queried, contested, and corrected has been built as weather, and logging alone does not fix that. This is the philosophical hinge of the essay and it is entirely absent from the public thirteen.

**"Authentic" disaggregated into seven non-equivalent questions** (factually sound, honestly disclosed, economically fair, personally expressive, culturally situated, difficult to make, aesthetically alive). Every combination can come apart. This forbids a single verdict axis and is the argument behind CC-1's refusal of a trust score, stated more generally than CC-1 states it.

**Ecological cost as a ledger field.** Compute budgets, efficiency targets, energy transparency, hardware longevity, repairability, smallest model that clears the criterion. This is an entire accounting dimension the public edition does not carry, and it arrives with its own self-check: local-first is not licence for every home to burn wastefully.

**The three author-adversarial tests, stated as falsifiable properties.** Catch its own author's dishonesty; survive his loss of control; keep working with his name removed. Sharper than "unable to be re-locked even by the author," because each is testable.

### Moderate bearing

- **Harm by construction.** A system needs no hostile intent, only a default configuration under which an ordinary person's presence registers as an error. Predicts that the most exclusionary systems pass every intent-based review.
- **The victim can inherit the robe.** The robe is structural, not an incumbent-power problem. A grievance channel that grants the complainant the criterion as well as the voice rebuilds the cage on the other side of the bench.
- **A true explanation used like a fake passport.** A correct systemic diagnosis is not an accounting for one's own conduct inside the system. Agent self-reports must not let situational analysis stand in for self-accounting.
- **Privacy is not concealment.** Withholding content does not require pretending the structure was never there. This is the exact justification for content-free provenance.
- **People are black boxes with excellent public relations.** Interpretability reframed as a rationalization problem: instrument the gap between a decision and the account given of it, which is checkable behaviourally without internals, and applies to the humans in the loop.
- **The productivity-gain distribution test.** Four days back is liberation, four people removed is extraction; the gain arrives with no moral owner installed. Gives a measurable success metric: time returned.
- **Redemption without erasure.** The record does not have to become the rest of a life. If only clean people qualify, redemption is another gate.
- **Multi-role contribution.** Framer, generator, solver, reconstructor, breaker. Record the parts. Single-author attribution on machine-assisted work is a schema defect, not a courtesy failure.
- **Anti-admiration test.** "If the work requires admiration, it is another insecurity machine." Applicable as a ship-time gate: strip the name and the praise surface, check whether it still functions.
- **Build the instrument, not the imitation.** Start lower than the prompt: primitives, laws, a variation source, a search method. The instrument may be the deepest part of the work.
- **Structural friction over stronger commitment.** Where intention has repeatedly failed to prevent a behaviour, the remedy is an obstacle in the path.
- **The unconformity method.** Read the layers above and below, the contact angle, comparable records elsewhere. Missing time still leaves conditions around the missingness. Naming the failure precisely: selecting the cause that serves the current case and calling inference memory.

### Low bearing, or explicitly little

- **The first third of the master.** Formation, "The first apology," the two heavily biographical sections. The extractions report that the argument in those sections is compressed into their closing passages and that the narrative bulk yielded nothing extractable. Do not send anyone back to mine it.
- **"The clock keeps moving."** One usable distinction (refusal on principle versus refusal to protect a role) in a purely rhetorical section.
- **The geology material,** apart from the unconformity method and the fossil-rarity corollary, is metaphor decoration with fences attached rather than argument.

## 2. Where the master is more qualified than the public edition

These are the highest-value findings in the report. Each is a place where the trim converted a bounded claim into an unbounded one.

| Public claim | Master's qualification | Consequence of the dropped fence |
|---|---|---|
| One-way valve: never train on unverified output. | Replay cannot manufacture coverage the system never encountered. New coverage comes from outside the line, and importing it is not contamination. | Without it, the valve reads as a closed loop and the system's asymptote looks like a virtue. |
| Verification throughput is a first-class budget. | The asymmetry can nullify a real result. Both "the model solved it" and "it is autocomplete" are rejected as status theater. | A metered budget still permits emitting more than can be checked. The master requires refusing, not just measuring. |
| Receipts certify lineage, not virtue. | Weights are not morally cursed. Foundation models used and disclosed are legitimate. Explicit refusal to build a new purity hierarchy. | Without it, provenance UI drifts into a clean/dirty binary, which is the robe with better typography. |
| Correctness comes from the loop. | The seams are witnesses, not foundations. A witness locates where the current model fails; none of them computes worth. | Without it, verdict counts become a quality score. |
| UNVERIFIABLE is a first-class verdict. | Say why. The epigenetics note models it: unverifiable because genetic, prenatal, ecological and cultural pathways cannot be separated. | A bare UNVERIFIABLE is unactionable and indistinguishable from "we did not look." |

## 3. Contradictions between master and public edition

One substantive tension, two of degree. None is a factual conflict.

**Substantive: permanence of the record versus permanence of the sentence.** Public point 4 makes the record append-only and forgiveness non-deleting. The master adds that the record does not have to become the rest of a life, and that if only clean people qualify, redemption is another gate. Both are the author's positions, and together they require something neither states: a policy for how age, repair, and subsequent verified behaviour weight a past failure in later decisions. The public edition, read alone, licenses a permanent adverse weight. The master, read alone, licenses forgetting. Neither is what the author means.

**Of degree: metering versus refusing.** Public point 10 treats verification capacity as a budget to display. The master treats exceeding it as output degradation. A budget you can knowingly overspend is not the master's position.

**Of degree: friction versus shame-free design.** Public point 13 says design for human variability, not shame. The master says put friction around the path that keeps winning. These resolve, and CC-1 has already resolved them in the phrase "strict truth, gentle consequences," but the resolution is not stated in either extraction: friction is structural and applies to the path, shame is punitive and applies to the person.

## 4. Design consequences for CC-1

Marked against the spec I read. Ordered by what the design does not yet have.

### Not covered

1. **Weighting of retained failure over time.** CC-1 has append-only, regression cases, typed invalidation, and both records kept. It has nothing on how an old FAIL weights a later decision. Concretely: a criterion or checker with a historical FAIL and a subsequent repair needs a defined standing, or the ledger becomes a permanent adverse record. Derives from redemption-without-erasure.
2. **Ecological and energy accounting.** The Layer 3 cost meter is multi-denominator and user-budgeted, but its denominators are verification-economic (attempts, oracle calls, tokens, cache hits). Energy, hardware longevity, and "smallest model that clears the criterion" are absent. The cost meter is the natural host and the field is cheap now, expensive later, which is the stated criterion for receipt v2 fields.
3. **Symmetric scrutiny on human gate decisions.** `forum` human gates are the only path to a human-adjudicated annotation and the promotion path from `gather` staging to the registry. The spec does not require a gate decision to emit a justification with the same evidentiary standing as a checker verdict. Per "people are black boxes with excellent public relations," the human decision in the accept path is currently the least instrumented node in the design.
4. **Coverage-gap reporting.** `gather` is a real external-intake channel with a human gate, which satisfies the flywheel-ceiling requirement structurally. What is missing is the measure: what the loop has never encountered. A system whose input is largely its own history should publish its coverage ceiling alongside its improvement curve. The Phase 1 base-rate survey is the nearest existing instrument.
5. **Explicit fork cost-accounting.** Exit rights are strong (Apache-2.0 split, offline, no account, open formats). "Cross-fork criterion reconciliation is unsolved" is already a named residual risk. The master adds the qualification that an exit which leaves verification burden behind is not sovereign, which sharpens that risk into a design question rather than an acknowledgment.
6. **Time returned as a reported metric.** Section 11's four claims are all epistemic. The productivity-distribution test suggests a fifth for the desktop surface. Arguably out of CC-1 scope; flagging it so the omission is deliberate.
7. **Non-normalized intake of a contest.** The contest channel exists and is the right shape. Whether a counter-receipt preserves the contestant's own framing alongside the schema-normalized record is unspecified. Normalization at intake is a mild form of editing the witness.

### Partially covered

8. **UNVERIFIABLE with a reason code.** The four-way verdict is in Layer 0 and UNVERIFIABLE is already first-class in `crucible`; `lanes.py` degrades an absent verifier to UNVERIFIABLE rather than a fake pass. Typed reason codes exist for INVALIDATION but not for UNVERIFIABLE. Distinguishable states: confounded, no witness available, outside the criterion's domain, lane absent. Cheap to add, and it is the difference between a labelled gap and a bounded one.
9. **Domain-of-applicability declaration.** CC-1 honours "a poem has no kernel" by construction: the oracle family is construction certificates and no aesthetic domain is in scope. The exposure is at the surface layer and adjacent tooling (prose contract and humanize capabilities exist in this environment). A criterion object should carry an explicit domain of applicability, and the renderer should refuse to display a verdict outside it.
10. **Repair re-checked under shift.** Principle 3 makes failed runs regression cases, and the retention probe already uses isomorphic perturbations to separate derivation from memorization. That machinery is exactly what a repaired case needs; the spec does not currently point it at repairs.
11. **Multi-role provenance.** Principle 6 specifies process manifests with roles. The human roles the master enumerates (framer, reconstructor, adversarial breaker) are not in the receipt v2 field list.
12. **Rights attaching to inputs.** CRITERION carries a license id. Input-level rights are not carried.
13. **Emission gated by checker throughput.** The cost meter caps and halts, and the construction-certificate choice makes verification cost negligible, which is a direct structural answer to review debt and worth stating as such in the spec's rationale. What is absent is a backlog surfaced as a first-class number when a checker is not negligible.
14. **The three author-adversarial tests as release gates.** Bar R already means "no trust in the author," and stranger re-derivation covers de-attribution and successor operation. Test one, catching the author's own dishonesty, is the weak leg, and the spec already knows it: "selective publication is detectable only as rollback, not as absence." That residual risk is the honest statement of the gap; naming the three tests as gates makes it a burn-down item rather than a standing caveat.
15. **Default-configuration exclusion audit.** "Runnable on machines people already own," T0 with no GPU, no network, no account, reproduce scripts in minutes on a laptop: the constraint is fully present. A standing audit asking which ordinary users the current defaults treat as an error condition is not.
16. **Purity-tier risk in the adapter layer.** "Any model runs; only carded, trainable models produce claim-admissible receipts" is a capability tier and is correct. The master's fence says do not let it render as clean versus dirty. This is a renderer requirement, not a schema change.

### Already covered, and covered well

17. **Criterion mutation.** Hash-pinned criterion objects, CRITERION_AMENDED as a recorded event with change_reason, parent hash, and downstream invalidation. This is the master's requirement implemented more completely than the essay asks.
18. **No aggregate score.** "No trust_score field exists anywhere. Only trust_recompute_cmd." This satisfies "the seams are witnesses, not foundations," the disaggregation of authentic, and the refusal of the artifact negotiating a better verdict on the author's behalf, in one line.
19. **Observed versus inferred, typed at the schema.** RAW OUTPUT hashed from the subprocess stream before Python reads it, `model_readout: false` structural, evidence_kind NOMINAL, input tier multiset never min()'d, specificational inputs recorded separately. This is a stronger implementation of the unconformity method than the essay articulates.
20. **Measurement boundary reported with the value.** `does_not_prove` required and non-empty with a mechanically derived floor, plus the coverage block on CertificateOracle and principle 11. Directly satisfies "those numbers are the portion the record could count."
21. **Exclusions as a receipt field.** DENOMINATOR carries tasks proposed and filtered, filter id and hash, and `filter_is_learned`. This is the master's "what was deliberately kept out" requirement, already present and sharper.
22. **Novelty as a relation with named ancestry.** NOVELTY verdict in {REDISCOVERY, NOT_FOUND_IN_CORPUS, UNKNOWN}, with "NOVEL" not a legal value and NOT_FOUND_IN_CORPUS naming its corpus. Exactly the master's redefinition.
23. **Pre-registration.** External anchor on freeze day, claim rule frozen verbatim, stopping rule as a fixed budget rather than "until significant," analysis script validated on synthetic data before the freeze.
24. **Structural friction where intention fails.** reward_gate writes, signs, chains, and commits the receipt before reward returns, with commit failure returning the UNDECIDED sentinel. The entropy guard uses hysteresis and preregistered abort thresholds. This is friction by construction rather than by resolve.
25. **Removal recorded as removal.** Harness-attributable failures are dropped to an exclusion ledger, not deleted; failures are recorded events, not corrupted state.
26. **Redaction preserving structure.** The `safe_read --audit-json` content-free provenance manifest, and bundle pack/verify with HMAC stripped at pack time.
27. **Contest as offset rather than reconciliation.** Signed counter-receipts, open contests published as a series, downstream records append and never mutate. This is the master's "recognize the displacement, do not press two records into one approved story," and it also answers the victim-inherits-the-robe problem correctly: a contestant gets standing and a fork, not authority over the incumbent criterion. Worth stating that explicitly in the spec, because it is currently true by construction rather than by rule.
28. **Instrument over imitation.** Generators with a difficulty knob, no instance proposer, a stranger regenerates the instance set from a seed, OpenEnv environment packages. The design already builds the instrument.
29. **Document-level evidence-class declaration.** The spec's own header (Status, Date, Provenance with run ids and links to the panel and research syntheses) is the pattern the master's content note models. Already convention.
30. **Author/artifact firewall.** "Binds this design as ethos and vocabulary, never as biography." Already the correct direction. The master adds the reverse leg, which the absence of any trust score already enforces.

## 5. What must not be carried into the product

Per the author's own fence, and the handling rules on this material.

1. **Any biography, at any density, on any product surface.** Not as an origin story, not as an about page, not as flavour in a README. The rule is bidirectional: the confession does not warrant the creation, and the creation does not absolve the creator. The operational test is that every artifact must be evaluable with the story removed. CC-1's "ethos and vocabulary, never as biography" is the correct binding and should be quoted in any surface review.
2. **Suffering as credential, in any direction.** The master makes this symmetric: it indicts critics who convert hours of difficulty into moral standing as sharply as it indicts the author. So no "built by someone who lived it," and equally no "hand-built, no shortcuts" register. Human-made does not automatically mean humane.
3. **Trauma and clinical vocabulary as system metaphor.** Fear extinction, relapse, dissociation, adverse childhood experience. These are analytic scaffolding inside the essay and each carries an explicit fence in the closing note. They must not become field names, verdict names, feature names, or UI copy. Use invalidation, regression case, re-verification, amendment.
4. **The moral and religious register.** Robe, bishop, confession, forgiveness, redemption, sin. Internal vocabulary only. On a verdict surface, "forgiveness" is a category error: the model is not being forgiven, a record is being amended.
5. **The human-model parallel itself.** The author's scope note bounds it: models are not people, are not assigned trauma, and forgiveness is not optimization. The parallel is a reasoning aid that produced the design. It is not an explanation to ship, and every place it appears on a surface invites the reading the author explicitly refuses.
6. **Doctrine as a condition of use.** The eight questions and the five-step credo are excellent internal review checklists. As public manifesto copy they violate the master's own instruction to reject the doctrine, and they convert a tool into a movement, which is the failure mode the author names in the same breath as the founder who becomes another bishop. Ship the mechanism; keep the creed in project-docs.
7. **Comparison and adversary framing.** The anti-authoritarian stance confers no legitimacy. CC-1 already bans comparison framing on public surfaces; the master supplies the reason, which is stronger than the convention.
8. **Third parties.** Named artists, works, family members, and the readings of them. The essay is explicit that artist biography was used to clarify voice and lineage and never as proof, and that the readings are interpretive. None of it belongs downstream of the essay.
9. **The master's rhetoric, as distinct from its arguments.** "Landfill," "insecurity machine," "small autocracy," "black boxes with excellent public relations." These are load-bearing in the essay and are hype in a spec. Carry the claim, drop the phrase.

The one thing from the closing apparatus that should be carried forward wholesale is its *form*, not its content: role-decomposed assistance disclosure, an honest null about what was not accessible in this run, per-metaphor fences, explicit non-claims where evidence is missing, dated verification status repeated wherever the claim appears, and internal repo paths cited at the same specificity as DOIs. That is a working specification for what a receipt on a knowledge artifact contains, and CC-1's receipt v2 already implements most of it for machine claims but not yet for prose deliverables.
