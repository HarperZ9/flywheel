# Competitive leadership after 1.0.0

Status: development direction, adopted September 17, 2026. This is an ambition
and an execution method, not a claim of current superiority or release completion.

## Objective

Compete to win across every relevant feature and aspect of the product. A
distinctive property is one strength, not a reason to cede ordinary capabilities
to another product. Research and iterative improvement apply to all development
and task work, including the environment that builds, evaluates and ships it.

Finish the existing 1.0.0 acceptance and release work first. After that release,
make competitive leadership the explicit next phase. The rule already governs
current choices; this sequencing prevents new expansion from displacing shipping.

## Compare the full experience

Maintain a feature comparison against the strongest relevant alternatives using
existing experiment and roadmap records. Cover at least:

- Capability, output quality and independently verified task completion.
- Reasoning and agent workflows, tool use, context retention and recovery.
- Latency, throughput, memory, compute, energy where measured, and cost per
  correct completion, including verification and retries.
- Reliability, interruption handling, persistence and failure diagnosis.
- Setup, usability, accessibility, documentation and cross-device operation.
- Model and provider choice, local operation, integration and portability.
- Privacy, permission enforcement, security and credential custody.
- Evaluation quality, re-derivation, observability and release/upgrade behavior.

Compare specialists on functions they support and full systems on matched
workflows. Use current versions and give baselines equivalent tools and harness
support. Record reported, reproduced and unknown evidence separately. Do not
select a weak comparator or a convenient narrow metric to declare a broad win.

## Convert gaps into shipped improvements

For each material gap, record the affected user workflow, current baseline,
strongest alternative, target, proposed mechanism, owner and next experiment.
Use the existing receipt and decision records rather than create a new platform.

Prioritize by user value, dependencies, uncertainty and implementation cost.
Deferred gaps retain a rationale and revisit trigger. Sequencing does not make
inferiority acceptable forever, and broad ambition does not require building
every feature or running every experiment simultaneously.

Research competing explanations and methods. Implement the strongest candidate,
then measure whether it improves the whole workflow. Include ordinary success,
false success, failure recovery and regression controls. Ship useful increments
and check the installed result. Keep a result that failed to improve the product
as evidence that changes the next attempt, not as a success claim.

For each iteration, improve both the product and, where worthwhile, the means
of improving it: a reusable benchmark, diagnostic, adapter, test fixture or
release check. Avoid extra tooling whose upkeep exceeds its demonstrated value.

## Evidence and decisions

The objective remains to win every relevant aspect. Claims of an actual win are
specific to the measured task, version, budget and conditions. Tradeoffs remain
visible; a speed gain cannot silently stand in for retained quality or safety.
Use the same standards for our work, competitors and potential partners.

Every evaluation must change a decision or lead to a checked improvement.
Record adoption, rejection, the next experiment or justified retention, with
the evidence and remaining uncertainty. Keep current release, authorization,
ownership and independent acceptance requirements in force.
