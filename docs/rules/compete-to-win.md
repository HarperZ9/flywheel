# Compete to win across every relevant feature

This rule applies to development, research, planning, evaluation and task work
in Flywheel. It was adopted on September 17, 2026.

## The rule

When Flywheel competes, the aim is leadership across every relevant feature and
every aspect of the experience. A distinctive property is one advantage to
develop. It never excuses losing on the common features, and a competitor's
present lead becomes a research target. Keep the
distinctive strengths and improve everything around them: capability, output
quality, speed, efficiency, reliability, usability, integration, portability,
privacy, security, evaluation and operations.

## How to apply it

- **Compare feature by feature.** Measure against the strongest relevant
  alternatives. Keep unknowns and measured deficits visible, our own included.
- **Turn each gap into work.** A material gap becomes a research question with
  competing explanations, a measurable target and an owned path to improvement.
  Study the methods behind the lead, reproduce the useful findings, improve the
  implementation and check the outcome.
- **Sequence by value.** Prioritize by user value, dependencies, uncertainty and
  cost. Putting a feature later in the order concedes nothing. A deferred gap
  keeps its reasoning and a trigger for revisiting it. Avoid inventing broad
  scope or running every experiment at once.
- **Test the whole experience.** Run ordinary workflows and hard cases against
  strong baselines that get equivalent support. Measure completed work, failures,
  recovery, setup, latency and resource cost. Improve the tools, benchmarks and
  release process that make the next improvement faster.
- **Hold the ambition and bound the claims.** The goal of winning every aspect
  stays, and each claim of superiority stays in proportion to its evidence. A
  present loss shapes the next iteration. It is neither a permanent surrender nor
  a result to hide.
- **Ship, verify, repeat.** Ship the improvement, verify it in real use, keep the
  regression controls and go again. Feature counts, promotional claims and
  favorable narrow benchmarks never substitute for a better product or for
  outcomes someone else has checked.

## Where Flywheel stands

The rule first asked for the 1.0.0 release to finish before competitive
leadership became the explicit next phase. Version 1.0.0 was tagged on
September 18, 2026, and later releases followed, so that phase is current work.
The working method, the comparison areas and the gap backlog format live in
[the competitive leadership plan](../../project-docs/plans/POST-1.0.0-COMPETITIVE-LEADERSHIP.md).

## Limits

The ambition carries no claim of present superiority. Any statement that Flywheel
leads on a feature needs its own comparison, baseline and evidence, under the
[evidence rule](evidence-and-useful-work.md).
